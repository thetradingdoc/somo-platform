Mission

We remove friction from healthcare revenue cycles. Somo agents collect the right data, explain balances in plain language, and deliver structured updates to EHRs, billing ledgers, and payer portals. The result: lower days in AR, happier callers, and compliant conversations you can trust.

Who We Serve

Patients

24/7 voice concierge that clarifies statements, verifies coverage, negotiates plans, and books payment commitments without long hold times.

Providers

Automated follow-ups for denials, prior auth, and collections. Every interaction syncs to the Somo dashboard and your practice systems.

Insurers

Structured data handoffs with eligibility facts, CPT/ICD context, and timestamped recordings ensure payers act on complete information.
