## 1. Definitions

- **Covered Entity** — The dental or medical practice subscribing to Kelly front desk services.
- **Business Associate (BA)** — Somo, which processes PHI on behalf of the Covered Entity via voice agent, eligibility verification, scheduling, and related portal features.
- **PHI** — Protected Health Information as defined under 45 CFR §160.103.
- **Services** — Kelly AI front desk (inbound voice, SMS), insurance eligibility (270/271), appointment scheduling, copay collection links, and provider dashboard.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures

BA may use and disclose PHI only to:

1. Perform Services under the MSA/subscription agreement.
2. Properly manage and administer BA's business, including quality assurance and legal compliance.
3. As required by law.

BA will not use PHI for marketing unrelated to the Services without written authorization.

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate

BA agrees to:

- Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this Agreement or required by law.
- Use appropriate safeguards (administrative, physical, technical) per 45 CFR §164.308, §164.310, §164.312.
- Report to Covered Entity any security incident or breach of unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay.
- Ensure subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI agree to the same restrictions (see vendor BAA checklist).
- Make PHI available to Covered Entity for access, amendment, and accounting as required by 45 CFR Part 164.
- Make internal practices, books, and records available to HHS for compliance determination.
- Return or destroy PHI upon termination where feasible; retain only as required by law or documented retention policy.

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity

Covered Entity agrees to:

- Provide BA only the minimum PHI necessary for the Services.
- Notify BA of any restrictions on use/disclosure of PHI.
- Obtain any patient authorizations required for BA's performance of Services (e.g., recording consent where applicable).

## 5. Term and termination

- **Effective date:** `[DATE]`
- **Term:** Co-terminous with the subscription / MSA.
- Upon termination, BA will return or destroy PHI per Section 3 within `[30]` days unless retention is required by law.
